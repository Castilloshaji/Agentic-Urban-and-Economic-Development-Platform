District 07 · Kerala
connecting

A working model of a district, not a dashboard about one.

Ninety seven local bodies. Twenty five measured parameters each, every one carrying the year it was published and who published it. Ask what a new industrial park does to Malayattoor and the answer arrives in under a second, because the answer is arithmetic.

Seven domain agents then argue about it. They read flood rasters, landslide classes, transit feeds, census counts, clinic and school locations, and the state's own grant formula. They can explain a verdict and they can disagree with each other. What they cannot do is move a weight or lift a hazard limit. That part is already settled.

02 The chaos Seven agents, one brief each, arguing inside their own budget envelope

What each domain put forward

Every proposal names the measured shortfall it targets. An envelope is money already committed to that domain, so an agent that proposes far below its envelope has to say why.

Agent analyses

Each agent reads your text, the measured position of every selected area, and your budget. The Budget agent additionally computes this area's share under Kerala's published devolution rule. None of them can change a weight or clear a constraint.
